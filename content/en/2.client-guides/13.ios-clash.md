Clash has no official iOS app. On iPhone and iPad, you use a third-party client that **supports Clash subscriptions**. None of these apps are in the mainland China App Store, so you'll need:

1. An Apple ID from a region outside mainland China, such as the US or Hong Kong;
2. To sign in to the App Store with that account and download a client;
3. A working Clash subscription link.

## Recommended clients

We recommend free open-source clients first. Their code is public, they cost nothing, and they import Clash subscriptions without trouble.

## Setup

::steps
### Set up an overseas Apple ID

We recommend registering your own Apple ID for another region with your own email address. You don't need to add a payment method to download free apps. Only use this account in the App Store, not under **Settings → iCloud**.

:::warning
Don't sign in to iCloud with a shared account that someone gave or sold you. The device could be locked remotely, or your private data could be exposed.
:::

### Download a client

Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, scroll to the bottom, sign out, and sign in with your overseas Apple ID. Then open one of the links above or search for the client by name. After installing, you can switch back to your usual account.

### Get your subscription link

The client doesn't include any servers, so you need a subscription from a provider. Many provider dashboards offer one-tap iOS import straight into the matching client.

![One-tap iOS import in a provider's dashboard](/images/showadrecket/page-clash-ios.webp)

:::note
Need a subscription? See [these subscription plans](https://iv.coolgo.me/aff.php?aff=35){target="_blank"} (third-party affiliate link; check the details before you buy).
:::

### Import and connect

Using Hiddify as an example: copy your subscription link, open the app, tap **New Profile → Add from clipboard**, then tap the button in the middle of the home screen to connect. Allow the VPN configuration when prompted the first time. See each client's guide for detailed steps.

## FAQ

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Why can't I find Clash in the App Store?"}
  Clash's original developer never released an iOS app, and these apps aren't listed in the mainland China App Store. Switch to an Apple ID from another region and search for a compatible client such as Hiddify or Clash Mi.
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Should I choose a free or a paid client?"}
  Free open-source Hiddify or Clash Mi is enough for most people. Consider paid apps such as Shadowrocket or Stash only if you need finer-grained routing rules, scripts, or other advanced features.
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="How do I buy paid apps with an overseas Apple ID?"}
  Top up the account with an App Store gift card for that region. Buy cards from official retailers; cheap cards from unknown sellers can get your account banned.
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="I can't get online after connecting."}
  Switch to a server with lower latency and make sure your subscription hasn't expired. If it still doesn't work, see [Troubleshooting](/en/installation-guide/troubleshooting).
  :::
::
