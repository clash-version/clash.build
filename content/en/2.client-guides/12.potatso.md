Potatso Lite (referred to as Potatso) is a network debugging tool on the iOS platform that supports various proxy protocols. It has a simple interface and is easy to use, making it suitable for beginners.

## Download and Install

Due to regional restrictions, Potatso Lite is not available in the App Store in mainland China. You need to use a non-Mainland China Apple ID (such as US or HK region) to download it.

## User Guide

### 1. Get Subscription
Potatso itself is just a tool and does not provide network services. You need to use it with a [`Provider (Subscription)`](/en/installation-guide/quick-start). If you are not sure how to choose one, please refer to [How to Choose the Right Provider↗](/en/installation-guide/quick-start).

### 2. Import Subscription

After obtaining the how-choose-service, there are usually several ways to import it:

#### Method 1: One-Click Import (Recommended)
On the provider's website, there is usually a button like "One-Click Import" or "Import to Potatso". Clicking it will automatically launch the App and import the configuration.

#### Method 2: Scan QR Code
1. Open the Potatso App.
2. Click **"Add a Proxy"** or the **"+"** sign in the top right corner.
3. Select **"QR Code"** and scan the QR code provided by the service provider.

#### Method 3: Manually Copy Link
1. Copy the **Subscription URL** from the provider's website.
2. Open the Potatso App and click **"Add a Proxy"** or the **"+"** sign.
3. Select **"Add URL"** (or Type: URI).
4. Paste the link into the URL bar, and you can give it a name (Remark).
5. Click **"Done"** to save.

### 3. Start Proxy

1. After successful import, you will see a new item in the configuration list.
2. Make sure the configuration you just imported is selected.
3. Click the blue play button (circular switch) in the bottom right corner.
4. **First Time Setup**: The system will verify permissions with "Potatso Lite Would Like to Add VPN Configurations". Please click **"Allow"**.
5. After verifying your fingerprint or password, the button will turn into a red stop icon, indicating a successful connection.

### 4. Smart Routing vs Global Proxy

Potatso usually has mode selection at the bottom:
- **Smart Routing**: Recommended. Automatically routes traffic based on rules; domestic websites connect directly, while foreign websites go through the proxy.
- **Global Proxy**: All traffic goes through the proxy. Usually used in specific cases (e.g., when some websites are not recognized correctly).
