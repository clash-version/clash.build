Potatso Lite is a free proxy client for iPhone and iPad with a simple interface, suited to people who only need the basics. It hasn't been updated in a long time, though, and its support for newer protocols is limited.

::note
Potatso Lite is **closed-source software**, so this site doesn't link to it for download. For broader protocol support, we recommend the free, open-source [Hiddify](/en/client-guides/hiddify) or [Clash Mi](/en/client-guides/clashmi).
::

## How to get it

Potatso Lite isn't available in the mainland China App Store. Sign in to the App Store with an Apple ID from another region, such as the US or Hong Kong, then search for **Potatso Lite** and install it. Afterwards, you can switch back to your usual Apple ID.

::warning
Only sign in to the overseas Apple ID in the App Store. Don't sign in to someone else's account under **Settings → iCloud**, or your device could be locked or your data exposed.
::

## Setup

::steps
### Get a subscription

Potatso Lite is only a client and does not include any servers. Get a subscription from your provider first.

:::note
Need a subscription? See [these subscription plans](https://iv.coolgo.me/aff.php?aff=35){target="_blank"} (third-party affiliate link; check the details before you buy). Treat your subscription link like a password and don't share it.
:::

### Import the subscription

Use any of these methods:

- **One-tap import**: tap the "Import to Potatso" (or similar) button in your provider's dashboard to open the app and import automatically.
- **Scan a QR code**: tap **+** in the app, choose **QR Code**, and scan the subscription QR code from your provider.
- **Add manually**: copy the subscription link, tap **+**, choose to add a URL, paste the link, enter a remark, and tap **Done**.

### Turn on the proxy

Make sure the subscription you just imported is selected, then tap the round switch in the bottom-right corner. The first time, iOS asks to add a VPN configuration. Tap **Allow** and confirm with your passcode or Face ID. When the switch changes to a stop icon, you're connected.

### Choose a routing mode

- **Smart Routing**: rule-based routing; recommended for daily use.
- **Global Proxy**: all traffic goes through the proxy; use it only temporarily when a specific site doesn't load correctly.
::

## FAQ

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Servers won't connect after importing."}
  Potatso Lite has limited support for newer protocols such as VLESS and Hysteria2. If your provider mainly uses them, switch to [Hiddify](/en/client-guides/hiddify) or [Clash Mi](/en/client-guides/clashmi).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="I can't get online after turning it on."}
  Switch to another server and make sure the routing mode is Smart Routing. If it still doesn't work, see [Troubleshooting](/en/installation-guide/troubleshooting).
  :::
::
