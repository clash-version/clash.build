## Installation Steps

### 1. Install the Application

#### App Self-Signing

**Step 1: Preparation**
1. A computer (Windows or Mac).

2. Data cable: Connect your phone to the computer.

3. Enable developer mode on your phone:

4. Go to Settings -> About Phone -> Tap Software Version 7 times quickly until you see "You are now in developer mode".

5. Return to Settings -> System -> Developer Options -> Enable USB Debugging.

**Step 2: Install Tools & Sideload**
Regular users are recommended to use community-developed automated installation tools that can automatically apply for free personal developer certificates and sign the app.

1. Download the installer:

Search and download "HarmonyOS Next Auto Installer" (also known as hap-installer, commonly found on GitHub by author likuai2010) on your computer.

2. Connect your phone:

Open the installer, connect your phone via data cable, and a "Allow USB debugging" popup will appear on your phone - tap Allow.

3. Log in to Huawei Account:

The tool usually requires you to log in to your Huawei account (to apply for a temporary debug certificate).

4. One-click installation:

Drag your ClashNEXT-LTS-1.5.1.hap file directly into the installer window.

Click Install/Sign. The tool will automatically complete signing and push the app to your phone.
