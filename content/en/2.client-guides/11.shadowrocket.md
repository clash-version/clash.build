Shadowrocket is one of the most widely used proxy clients for iPhone and iPad. It supports Clash subscriptions and most mainstream protocols, has powerful rule-based routing, and is fairly easy to use.

::note
Shadowrocket is **closed-source paid software**, so this site doesn't link to it for download. If you'd prefer a free open-source client, try [Hiddify](/en/client-guides/hiddify) or [Clash Mi](/en/client-guides/clashmi). Both are free on the App Store.
::

## How to get it

Shadowrocket isn't available in the mainland China App Store. You'll need an Apple ID from another region, such as the US or Hong Kong:

1. In the App Store, tap your profile picture, sign out, and sign in with your overseas Apple ID.
2. Search for **Shadowrocket**, then buy and install it. There are many look-alike apps with similar names, so check the name and icon carefully.
3. After installing, you can switch back to your usual Apple ID. Installed apps keep working.

::warning
Only sign in to the overseas Apple ID in the App Store. Don't sign in to someone else's account under **Settings → iCloud**, or your device could be locked or your data exposed.
::

## Setup

::steps
### Get a subscription

Shadowrocket is only a client and does not include any servers. Get a subscription from your provider first.

:::note
Need a subscription? See [these subscription plans](https://iv.coolgo.me/aff.php?aff=35){target="_blank"} (third-party affiliate link; check the details before you buy). Treat your subscription link like a password and don't share it.
:::

### Import the subscription

**Option 1: one-tap import (recommended).** Sign in to your provider's dashboard in your phone's browser, open the import section, and choose **iOS → Shadowrocket**. Confirm the redirect and the subscription is added automatically.

![One-tap import to Shadowrocket from a provider's dashboard](/images/showadrecket/install1.webp)

**Option 2: add it manually.** Copy the subscription link and open Shadowrocket. The app usually detects the clipboard and offers to add it. If it doesn't, tap **+** in the top-right corner, set the type to **Subscribe**, paste the link into URL, and tap **Done**.

### Turn on the proxy

Select a server on the home screen and turn on the connection switch at the top. The first time, iOS asks to add a VPN configuration. Tap **Allow** and confirm with your passcode or Face ID. When the VPN icon appears in the status bar, you're connected.

![Turn on the connection switch in Shadowrocket](/images/showadrecket/install2.webp)
::

## Advanced settings

### Global routing

**Global Routing** on the home screen controls where traffic goes:

- **Config**: rule-based routing. Local sites connect directly and international sites go through the proxy. Use this day to day.
- **Proxy**: all traffic goes through the proxy. Use it only temporarily when a specific site doesn't load correctly.
- **Direct**: no traffic goes through the proxy.

### Update and test servers

Pull down on the home screen to update the subscription. Tap **Connectivity Test** to check each server's latency, and choose one with low latency.

## FAQ

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="One-tap import doesn't open Shadowrocket."}
  Make sure Shadowrocket is installed and that you opened your provider's dashboard in Safari. You can also add the subscription manually.
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="I can't get online after turning it on."}
  Switch to a server with lower latency, make sure Global Routing is set to Config, and pull down on the home screen to update the subscription. See [Troubleshooting](/en/installation-guide/troubleshooting) for more.
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Is there a free alternative?"}
  Yes. [Hiddify](/en/client-guides/hiddify) and [Clash Mi](/en/client-guides/clashmi) are free, open-source, available on the App Store, and both support Clash subscriptions.
  :::
::
