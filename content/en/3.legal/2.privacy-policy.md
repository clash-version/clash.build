## Scope

This policy applies to information processed when you visit Clash.Build. When you follow a link to GitHub, an app store, a project website, or a download provider, that third party's privacy policy applies.

## Information we process

Clash.Build currently does not provide user registration, payments, comments, or proxy subscription services. We do not ask visitors to submit proxy subscription URLs, configuration files, passwords, or payment information.

To deliver pages, protect the site, and diagnose errors, the server, hosting provider, or CDN may automatically process technical information such as:

- IP address and approximate network region;
- browser, device, and operating-system type;
- access time, requested URL, referring page, and response status;
- security events and technical error logs.

The site may store interface preferences, such as language or color mode, in your browser. These preferences support site functionality and are not used to sell personal information or build cross-site advertising profiles.

## Purposes of processing

Technical information may be used to:

- deliver and improve site content;
- identify errors, abuse, and security threats;
- understand aggregate page usage;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Third-party services and links

The site may rely on hosting, CDN, image-processing, and source-code hosting services. It also links to GitHub, app stores, and third-party project websites. Those providers may process IP addresses, cookies, or device information under their own policies. Clash.Build does not control or guarantee third-party privacy practices.

## Retention and disclosure

Technical-log retention depends on security needs, troubleshooting requirements, and hosting settings and should be limited to a reasonable period. We do not intentionally disclose identifying information except when required to operate the site, prevent abuse, comply with law, or protect legitimate rights.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the site and technical logs, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not publish passwords, subscription URLs, configuration files, or other sensitive information in public issues, URL parameters, or feedback channels.

## Children's privacy

The site provides general technical documentation and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If inappropriate information is identified, we will take reasonable action after verification.

## Your choices and rights

You can clear cookies, local storage, and cached site data through your browser. Questions or applicable requests concerning information directly processed by Clash.Build may be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/clash-version/clash-download/issues). We may need to verify the scope and identity of a request for security reasons.

## Policy changes

If site functionality or data-processing practices change, this policy and its revision date will be updated.
